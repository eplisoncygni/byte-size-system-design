How they connect

The thread running through it all

These aren't nine isolated topics — they interlock. Write-ahead logging (Day 4) is the durability layer beneath LSM trees (Day 5), whose in-memory memtable is a skip list (Day 8) and whose on-disk files are guarded by Bloom filters (Day 2).

Consistent hashing (Day 1) decides where data lives across a cluster; Merkle trees (Day 3) check whether replicas match; Raft (Day 7) is that same durable log from Day 4 taught to survive the loss of whole machines. And the two probabilistic structures bookend the set: Bloom answers "is X in the set?", HyperLogLog (Day 9) answers "how big is the set?"

Union-Find (Day 6) is the pure-algorithm palate cleanser — the recurring reminder that a great data structure often comes down to one clever, simple idea.
